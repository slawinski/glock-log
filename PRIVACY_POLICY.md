# TriggerNote Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 2, 2026

TriggerNote ("the App") is a personal firearms, ammunition, and range visit logging application. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App handles, how it is stored, and what is never done with it.

## 1. Data the App Handles

When you use the App, you may enter or attach the following information:

- **Firearms inventory** — make, model, caliber, serial number, purchase date, photos, and personal notes.
- **Ammunition stock** — caliber, brand, grain, quantity, purchase date, price, and notes.
- **Range visit logs** — dates, locations, firearms used, ammunition used, round counts, and notes.
- **Photos** — images you attach to firearms, ammunition, or range visits (including any embedded metadata such as GPS location in those photos).
- **App settings** — currency preference and lock preferences.

## 2. Storage — Everything Stays On Your Device

**All data is stored exclusively on your device.** TriggerNote has no accounts, no servers, and no cloud sync.

- Data is stored locally using an encrypted storage engine (AES-256 encryption) with the encryption key protected by the device's secure keychain.
- Photos are stored in the App's private, on-device directory.
- The App can be configured to require Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode before data is displayed.

## 3. What the App Never Does

- **No data leaves your device automatically.** The App does not transmit your data anywhere, for any purpose.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no ads.** The App contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking of any kind.
- **No third-party sharing.** None of your data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.

## 4. Permissions

- **Photo Library:** The App requests access to your photo library solely so you can attach your own photos to your entries. Photos are copied into the App's private storage and are never uploaded.
- **Face ID / Touch ID:** The App may use biometric authentication to protect access to your data. Biometric data itself is processed only by Apple's secure hardware and is never accessible to the App.

## 5. Export and Backup (User-Initiated Only)

The App provides a Data Transfer feature that lets you export your data (including attached images) as a file, or import a previously exported file. Exports and imports occur only when you explicitly initiate them, and you choose where the exported file is stored or shared (for example, via the iOS share sheet). TriggerNote is not responsible for the security of exported files once they leave the App.

## 6. Data Deletion

You can delete individual entries (firearms, ammunition, range visits, and their images) at any time within the App, or erase all App data at once using the "Clear All Data" option. Deleting the App from your device also removes all App data.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children. The App is rated 17+ on the App Store.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this location, with a new effective date.
